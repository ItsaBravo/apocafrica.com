About Our Mission

APOC is dedicated to combating animal poaching and protecting endangered wildlife through innovative solutions, community engagement, and international cooperation.

Protection

We protect endangered animals from illegal poaching through technology, law enforcement, and community engagement.

Conservation

Preserving biodiversity and ecosystems by supporting anti-poaching initiatives across continents.

Community

Building partnerships with local communities to create sustainable solutions against wildlife trafficking.

Why APOC AFRICA?

Created by Amani Duma, APOC AFRICA combines artificial intelligence with real-world conservation efforts. We use cutting-edge technology and data-driven strategies to identify poaching hotspots, support ranger patrols, and reduce demand for illegal wildlife products.

Global Impact

Our initiatives have protected thousands of endangered animals and engaged millions in wildlife conservation worldwide. From African elephants to Asian tigers, we're making a difference.

Our Projects

NEXUS manages multiple conservation projects focused on protecting the world's most endangered species from poaching and illegal trade.

Elephant Protection

Save African elephants from ivory poaching with ranger patrols, GPS tracking technology, and demand reduction campaigns across Asian markets.

  • 20,000+ elephants at risk annually
  • GPS collar tracking systems
  • Anti-poaching ranger patrols
  • Ivory trade bans

Rhino Conservation

Protect critically endangered rhinos through intensive anti-poaching efforts, habitat restoration, and international horn trade bans.

  • 500+ rhinos poached yearly
  • Horn dehorning programs
  • Intensive protection patrols
  • Demand reduction in Asia

Tiger Protection

Combat tiger poaching for bones, skins, and organs used in traditional medicine through reserve protection and ranger expansion.

  • Only 3,900 wild tigers remain
  • Protected reserve expansion
  • Consumer education campaigns
  • Traditional medicine alternatives

Habitat Restoration

Restore natural habitats to provide safe spaces for poached species recovery and wildlife population growth.

  • Habitat protection initiatives
  • Ecosystem restoration
  • Wildlife corridor creation
  • Community-based conservation
